Camera Access Needed

PulseFusionNet reads your pulse through the rear camera lens — everything is processed on this device, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Ready when you are

60-second on-device pulse scan

How it works
Open the scanner

Tap Start Measurement to activate the rear camera.

Cover the lens

Rest your fingertip flat over the camera, flash needed only if ambient light is low.

Hold steady

Keep still for 60 seconds while the signal stabilizes.

Read your result

The on-device model returns your BPM and confidence.

Scanning Guidance
Reliable Range: 54 – 135 BPM

Optimized for still resting measurement on smartphone camera optical sensors.

Post-Exercise Tip (Wait 3 Mins)

After heavy exercise, rest for 3 minutes before measuring for your pulse to stabilize.

Elevated Heart Rate Tip

If your heart felt fast during measurement, wait 3 minutes and re-measure while sitting comfortably.

General wellness reference only — not a medical device.

Tips for accurate reading

        
Keep your finger flat and still on the lens

        
Apply gentle, steady pressure — not too hard

        
Rest your arm on a surface to reduce motion

        
Avoid scrolling or tapping during measurement

        
If outdoors, shade the camera from direct sunlight
